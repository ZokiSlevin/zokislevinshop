About this spreadsheet

Home Maintenance Tracker Spreadsheet for everyday record keeping

Home maintenance is easy to postpone when information is scattered across receipts, emails, appliance manuals and calendar reminders. A structured maintenance tracker gives you one place to record service work and plan recurring tasks.

Use the spreadsheet for jobs such as HVAC service, filter replacement, inspections, plumbing, electrical work, roof maintenance, appliance servicing and other recurring or one-time home projects. Record when work was completed, what it cost, who performed it and when the next service is expected.

The workbook can also serve as a long-term service history for the property. Over time, this makes it easier to remember previous repairs, contractor details and warranty information when an appliance or system needs attention again.

Can I add my own maintenance categories?

Yes. The spreadsheet is designed to be edited with your own home-maintenance tasks, categories and records.

Can it track appliance warranties?

Yes. Appliance, equipment and warranty information can be recorded alongside maintenance and repair history.

Can I use it for recurring maintenance?

Yes. The tracker is intended to help manage recurring maintenance and next-due dates as well as one-time repairs.

Is Excel required?
