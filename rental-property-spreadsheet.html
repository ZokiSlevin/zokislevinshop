About this spreadsheet

Rental Property Spreadsheet for everyday record keeping

A rental property can generate multiple streams of information at the same time: rent payments, utilities, repairs, maintenance, insurance, fees, tenant details and lease dates. Keeping these details in separate notes or documents makes it harder to see how a property is actually performing. This spreadsheet brings those records together in a structured Excel workbook.

Use the workbook to record rental income and recurring or one-time property expenses, then review totals and cash-flow information in one place. Property and tenant information can be kept alongside financial records, which makes the spreadsheet useful both for ongoing tracking and for preparing information for a more detailed review later.

The template is intended for small landlords, individual property owners and anyone who wants a straightforward rental-property tracker without a monthly software subscription. Microsoft Excel is recommended for the best experience, and many parts of the workbook may also work in Google Sheets with some formatting differences.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I use the spreadsheet for more than one rental property?

Yes. The workbook is designed to organize property-level records and can be adapted for multiple rental properties depending on how you choose to structure your data.

Does it calculate rental cash flow?

The workbook is designed to summarize income and expenses so you can review rental-property cash flow and related financial totals.

Is this accounting software?

No. It is a practical spreadsheet tracker for organizing rental-property records. It does not replace professional accounting, tax or legal advice.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Microsoft Excel is recommended for the best formatting and formula experience. Many spreadsheet elements may also work in Google Sheets, although some formatting can display differently.
