About this spreadsheet

Mileage Tracker Spreadsheet for everyday record keeping

Business driving can become difficult to reconstruct if trips are not recorded consistently. A mileage tracker gives you one structured place to log where you traveled, why the trip was business-related and how far you drove.

The workbook can also record mileage reimbursement and additional trip costs such as tolls and parking. This makes it useful for freelancers, self-employed workers, consultants, delivery work and small businesses that need a simple travel record.

This spreadsheet is an organizational tool. Tax treatment, deductible mileage rules and reimbursement rates vary by country and situation, so users should apply the rates and rules that are appropriate for them.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I enter my own mileage reimbursement rate?

Yes. The spreadsheet is intended to work with the rate that applies to your own situation.

Can I track tolls and parking?

Yes. Trip-related costs such as tolls and parking can be recorded alongside mileage information.

Is this a tax calculator?

No. It is a mileage and trip-record spreadsheet. Tax rules and deductible mileage requirements depend on your jurisdiction.

Can I use it for multiple vehicles?

The workbook can be adapted to distinguish vehicles or trip records depending on how you want to organize your data.
