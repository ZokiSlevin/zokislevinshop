Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit this website or submit a custom project inquiry to ZokiSlevinShop.

Information you provide

If you use the contact or custom project form, you may provide information such as your name, email address, project type, project description, preferred file type and requested timeline. You may also contact ZokiSlevinShop directly by email.

How information is used

Information submitted through the website is used only to review and respond to your inquiry, discuss a requested project, provide customer support, and maintain reasonable business records where necessary.

Contact form provider

The website contact form is configured to use FormSubmit, a third-party form-processing service, to deliver form submissions to zmacesic@gmail.com. Information submitted through the form may therefore be processed by FormSubmit in order to transmit your message. If you prefer not to use the form, you can contact ZokiSlevinShop directly by email.

Etsy

Product purchase buttons may direct you to the ZokiSlevinShop Etsy storefront. Etsy is a separate third-party platform with its own privacy practices, terms and policies. Purchases, payments and Etsy account information are handled by Etsy and its service providers.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the website does not intentionally include advertising trackers or website analytics scripts. If analytics, advertising tools or additional third-party services are added later, this policy and any necessary consent controls should be updated accordingly.

Data retention

Inquiry information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, manage any resulting project, maintain relevant business records and comply with applicable obligations.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: zmacesic@gmail.com.

This page is a practical website privacy notice and is not legal advice. If the site later adds analytics, newsletters, payment processing, customer accounts or other data-processing services, the policy should be reviewed and updated.
