SEARCH CONSOLE PRODUCT SNIPPET FIX - 2026-09-09

Issue reported by Google Search Console:
"Either offers, review or aggregateRating should be specified" for Product snippets.

Cause:
Five spreadsheet guide pages contained schema.org Product JSON-LD without offers, review or aggregateRating.

Fix applied:
- Removed Product structured data from the five guide pages.
- Replaced it with WebPage + CreativeWork structured data.
- Kept BreadcrumbList and FAQPage structured data unchanged.
- Kept the Etsy BuyAction as the external purchase destination.
- No ratings/reviews were fabricated.
- No Etsy prices were hardcoded, avoiding stale price markup when Etsy prices or discounts change.

Updated pages:
- /home-inventory-spreadsheet
- /home-maintenance-tracker
- /inventory-tracker-spreadsheet
- /mileage-tracker
- /rental-property-spreadsheet

Validation performed:
- No @type Product JSON-LD remains in the project.
- All JSON-LD blocks parse successfully.
