About this spreadsheet

Home Inventory Spreadsheet for everyday record keeping

A household can contain hundreds of items, yet most people do not have a clear record of what they own, where it is located or what it would cost to replace. A home inventory spreadsheet provides a simple way to build that record over time.

Use the workbook to list furniture, appliances, electronics, equipment, jewelry, tools and other belongings. Add purchase or replacement values, note whether receipts or other proof are available and keep relevant insurance information close to the inventory record.

The spreadsheet is designed as an organizational tool rather than an insurance valuation service. Values and documentation should be based on your own records and any requirements provided by your insurer.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this an insurance policy template?

No. It is a household inventory spreadsheet that can help organize item and policy-related information.

Can I add receipts or photos?

The spreadsheet can record whether supporting proof exists and can be adapted to include file references or links depending on your workflow.

Can renters use it too?

Yes. A household inventory can be useful for renters as well as homeowners.

Can I customize the categories?

Yes. The workbook is designed to be edited with categories and details that fit your household.
