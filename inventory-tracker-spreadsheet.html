About this spreadsheet

Inventory Tracker Spreadsheet for everyday record keeping

Small businesses often outgrow simple handwritten stock lists but do not necessarily need a full inventory-management platform. This spreadsheet fills the gap by keeping the most important stock records in an organized workbook that is easy to update.

Record products, quantities, stock movements, suppliers and purchase information, then use reorder levels to highlight items that require attention. This creates a more repeatable process for checking stock before it becomes a problem.

The spreadsheet can also help you review inventory value and potential revenue based on the information you enter. It is useful for small shops, makers, online sellers and service businesses that keep products, materials or supplies on hand.

Perfect for

  • Small online shops
  • Makers and handmade businesses
  • Retail inventory tracking
  • Supply and materials management
  • Businesses that want simple reorder control

FAQ

Common questions

Does the spreadsheet show low-stock items?

It is designed to track reorder levels and help identify items that need replenishment.

Can I track suppliers?

Yes. Supplier and vendor information can be kept alongside inventory and purchase-order records.

Can I use it for raw materials instead of finished products?

Yes. The workbook can be adapted for products, materials, supplies or other inventory items.

Does it replace an inventory-management system?

No. It is a practical spreadsheet solution for smaller workflows that do not require a full inventory-management platform.
